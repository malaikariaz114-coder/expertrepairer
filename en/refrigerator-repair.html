What we maintain

Is your fridge showing these problems?

Fridge problems often start with small signs, so don't wait until your food spoils.

Weak cooling

What we do

We check the compressor, thermostat and refrigerant and pinpoint the cause.

Water leaks

What we do

We clear blocked drain lines and fix leaks inside and under the fridge.

Excessive ice build-up

What we do

We service the defrost system, heaters and sensors.

Strange or loud noises

What we do

We inspect the evaporator fan, condenser and compressor and fix the source.

Door won't seal properly

What we do

We replace the door gasket and adjust hinges to stop cold air escaping.

Control panel not working

What we do

We check the electronics, thermostat and sensors.
