What we maintain

Common washing machine problems we fix

If you notice any of these, your washing machine needs repair.

Water leaking

What we do

We check hoses, seals and the drain filter and fix the source of the leak.

Not spinning or wringing

What we do

We check the motor, drive belt and carbon brushes and get it running again.

Strong vibration and noise

What we do

We rebalance the machine and check the mounts and loose parts.

Slow filling or draining

What we do

We clean the pump and filters and check the water valves.

Control panel faults

What we do

We test the buttons, display, sensors and electronic board.

Door won't open or close

What we do

We check the door lock, hinges and seal and adjust them.
